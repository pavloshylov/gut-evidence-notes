Privacy Policy

App: Gut Evidence Notes Poster · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Gut Evidence Notes Poster is a small private tool used by one person, the owner of the Threads account @ghroftkron (“Gut Evidence Notes”), to publish that account’s own posts through the Threads API. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up for other people.

What the app accesses

  • The profile ID and username of the account owner, after the owner explicitly authorises the app in Threads.
  • The ability to publish posts and replies on the owner’s own account.
  • Publicly available Threads posts and replies (text, link, author username, time) that the owner looks up to find conversations that relate to gut-health topics.

What the app does with it

  • Access tokens and any retrieved public posts are stored only on the owner’s own computer or in the owner’s private storage.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to build profiles of other people.
  • The app does not collect data from visitors of this website. This page does not use cookies or trackers.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the app’s access at any time in Threads under Settings → More settings → Website permissions. After revocation the stored access token is deleted. See the data deletion instructions.
