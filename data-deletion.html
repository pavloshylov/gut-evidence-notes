Data Deletion Instructions

App: Gut Evidence Notes Poster · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Gut Evidence Notes Poster only stores an access token for the Threads account of its owner (@ghroftkron) and publicly available posts the owner looked up for research. It holds no account data about other people.

To remove the app’s access

  1. Open Threads and go to Settings → More settings → Website permissions.
  2. Under “Active”, find Gut Evidence Notes Poster and remove it.

After removal the access token stops working and the owner deletes the stored copy.

To request deletion of anything related to you

If you believe the app holds information about you (for example, a public post of yours that was retrieved during research), email ghroftkron@gmail.com with a link to the post or your Threads username. It will be deleted and confirmed by email.

See also the Privacy Policy.
